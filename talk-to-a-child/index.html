How to talk to a child on Oiê

Five things, with examples. The child in the examples is called Tito.

1. Write to the mum’s or dad’s WhatsApp.

The child uses their number. You don’t install anything. The child’s messages arrive with their name in front:

  1. Tito: Tito: Hi Grandma, how are you?

2. Start with the name.

That’s how Oiê knows the message is for the child.

  1. You: Tito, want to come over on Saturday?Reaches the child
  2. You: Hi Tito, good night.Reaches the child
  3. You: Is Tito free on Saturday?Stays with the parents

3. Or answer what they sent.

For 24 hours after the child writes, you don’t need the name.

  1. Tito: Tito: I scored a goal today
  2. You: Brilliant, tell me everythingReaches the child

4. In a group, everyone sees everything.

In a group the parents allowed, the child sees everything the group says, from everyone. To talk only to the parents, message them privately.

  1. Cousins group
  2. Cousins group: Grandma: Cake here on SundayThe child sees it
  3. Tito: Tito: I’m coming

5. The parents see everything.

It’s their WhatsApp. What you send the child, they see too.

Good to know

  • Only people the parents put on Oiê’s list can talk to the child. If you’re not sure, ask them.
  • Text, voice, photos, videos and stickers arrive. Documents and locations don’t.
  • During school and bedtime, messages arrive without sound.
